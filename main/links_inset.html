Recommended Media

Comics

Raccoon Girl
Superhero Webcomic
RACCOON-GIRL.CO.UK
nohua's take

On the surface this comic doesn't seem like it works within the themes our comic is telling, but if anything? It's talking about the same thing, but in a much more "family friendly" way that is a little more digestible for an audience that might not be used to casual child abuse and societal expecations.

Skarik says, "r.i.p. raccoon man"

One Time Taxes
Snappy Webcomic
ONETIMETAXES.THE-COMIC.ORG
nohua's take

Similar to Raccoon Girl but ascending up a level of media literacy, One Time Taxes innundates you with a life that is basically normal to some people. Sure there is bombastic cartoony shit happening but - the analogy is still society wasn't made for you.

skarik's take

Some good fucking moment-to-moment pacing that's less standard dragging webcomic and more something that doesn't waste the authors' time or your time.

Poetics of Space
Philosphy
skarik's take

I like how it codifies moments of emotion. Like when you get a pang of nostalgia. Or you cry at a movie. It's fun to struggle through. Good paired with modern horror.

Games

MOLE
ITCH.IO
nohua's take

The story could be better, but it's aesthetically supreme and there's no actual monster (there is but it's not the point). It drips with cultural relevance to the creators - and if you want to understand it you have to think.

skarik's take

The moment-to-moment is absolutely great for another "do a fucking job" sim horror. Story is so-so but I adore the tunnel vision of the "protaganist." Go. Dig a hole for the good of mankind. You have to. See the sunlight.

fathomless
ITCH.IO
skarik's take

Short little read. If you're like me, you don't gotta directly relate to the narrator here to feel the heavy brush strokes of US history fucking your heritage with the hardwood end of the brush. Or maybe you're angry you don't relate at all because you're so removed. Good.

nohua's take

I might not be off the boat culturally but I resonate with this because of the lack of culture I do have - and yet I relate to this exact feeling in some ways. The requirements one has to have to make capital in the united states extends to everyone. I'm black - but was made a criminal from birth.
